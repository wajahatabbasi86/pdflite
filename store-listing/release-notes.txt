First release of TrenDoc:
• Merge PDFs, Split/Extract pages, Compress, Image(s) → PDF, PDF → Image(s), and a PDF viewer with pinch-to-zoom
• Fill Forms for fillable PDFs, and Add Text for scanned forms with no fillable fields
• Open any PDF directly from your file manager's "Open with" menu
• Pick your own accent color and background style in Appearance
• One small banner ad; optionally remove it for 1 day with a one-off purchase (no subscription), or for 2 hours by watching a rewarded video
