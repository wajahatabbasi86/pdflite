Ever open a "free" PDF app just to read one document, and instead get a pop-up, a "your file is out of date" warning, a welcome screen, or a paywall before you've even seen your file? TrenDoc doesn't do that. Pick a tool, do the job, get your file. That's the entire app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Merge PDFs — combine multiple documents into one, in the order you choose
• Split / Extract pages — pull out specific pages or burst a document into ranges
• Compress PDF — shrink file size by re-encoding embedded images, with an honest before/after size (never a fabricated number)
• Image(s) → PDF — turn photos or scans into a PDF, one page per image
• PDF → Image(s) — export pages as high-resolution JPG or PNG
• View PDF — open and read any PDF, including straight from your file manager's "Open with" menu

NOTHING BETWEEN YOU AND YOUR FILE
• No pop-ups, no "welcome" screens, no fake system warnings, no interruptions — ever, on any screen
• No account or sign-up, so there's nothing to crash into and nothing to log in to
• No subscription. Ever. If removing the one small ad is worth it to you, it's a single one-time payment — not a recurring charge, not a free trial that quietly needs a card number first
• No watermark on any output, whether you've paid or not
• One small banner ad on the home screen only. No interstitials, no video ads, no ad breaks mid-task

WHERE YOUR FILES ACTUALLY GO: NOWHERE ELSE
• Every file is processed entirely on your device — nothing is ever uploaded to a server, including compression and conversion
• TrenDoc shares nothing with anyone. Not app activity, not device identifiers, not analytics — there's no tracking SDK in this app to share it with
• Uses Android's Storage Access Framework, so TrenDoc never asks for broad storage permissions — you choose exactly which file it can see, one file at a time

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone who needs to quickly merge a few PDFs before emailing them, pull one page out of a long document, shrink a scan-heavy file before uploading it, or turn a stack of photos into a single PDF — without wading through ads, accounts, or a paywall to do it.

TrenDoc keeps a short, focused list of tools done well, rather than a long list of tools half-locked behind a subscription. If you've ever uninstalled a "free" PDF app because of what it put between you and your document, this is built for you.
