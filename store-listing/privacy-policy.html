Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026 Applies to package com.trendoc.pdflite

TrenDoc merges, splits, compresses, and converts PDFs entirely on your device. This page explains, plainly, what that means for your data — most of which is that there isn't any to explain, since your files never leave your phone.

01The short version

TrenDoc does not have a server, an account system, or a database of user files. Every merge, split, compression, and conversion happens in memory on your own device using Android's built-in PDF renderer and the open-source PdfBox-Android library, and the output stays exactly where you tell it to be saved.

Never collected

The content of your PDFs or photos, their file names, or anything about what you do with them.

Stored only on your device

Your Appearance preferences (accent color, theme) and whether you've purchased Remove Ads — both in local app storage, never transmitted anywhere.

Handled by Google, not TrenDoc

Advertising identifiers (AdMob) and purchase records (Play Billing) — see sections 5 and 6.

02Your documents & photos

TrenDoc opens files exclusively through Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) — the system file picker you already use for Google Drive, Files, or any other app. When you pick a file, Android grants TrenDoc a one-time handle to that specific file; TrenDoc never requests broad storage access, never scans your device for other files, and never learns your folder structure.

Processing (merging, splitting, compressing, rendering, converting) happens entirely in the app's own memory and temporary cache. The result is written back out only through another SAF picker — a location you choose, every time. At no point does a file, a thumbnail, or any text extracted from a PDF get sent over the network. TrenDoc's INTERNET permission (see §3) is used solely by the advertising and billing SDKs described below — the file-processing code itself never calls it.

03App permissions

TrenDoc requests only two Android permissions, both declared in its manifest:

PermissionUsed for
INTERNET Fetching the AdMob banner ad and communicating with Google Play Billing. Not used by any PDF/image processing code.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Letting the AdMob SDK check connectivity before requesting an ad, so it can fail quietly rather than hang.

TrenDoc does not request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive runtime permission.

04What's stored on your device

TrenDoc keeps a small amount of app preference data in local, private app storage (Android Jetpack DataStore) — the same mechanism any app uses to remember a setting. None of it is synced to a server, an account, or any other device.

Stored valuePurpose
Accent color, card/border/muted tint, background style, Home layout, theme (light/dark/system) Remembers your Appearance choices between launches.
Whether "Remove Ads" has been purchased Lets TrenDoc skip the ad banner without re-checking Google Play every time the app opens.

Uninstalling TrenDoc deletes all of it, along with everything in the app's temporary processing cache.

05Advertising (Google AdMob)

TrenDoc shows a single small banner ad on the Home screen, served by Google AdMob. To do this, the AdMob SDK running inside TrenDoc communicates with Google's ad servers and may collect an advertising identifier, coarse device and app information, and general engagement signals (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped), governed by Google's own privacy policy. TrenDoc itself never sees or stores this data — it passes directly between your device and Google.

No ad is shown anywhere except the Home screen; there are no interstitial or rewarded ads in TrenDoc.

06Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional, one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play's billing system — TrenDoc never collects or sees your payment method, card details, or billing address. Google shares back only a purchase confirmation, which TrenDoc uses to flip the local "ads removed" flag described in §4. Refunds, payment history, and receipts are managed through your Google Play account, not through TrenDoc.

07Data we share

TrenDoc has no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising network of its own. The only data that leaves your device is what's described in §5 and §6 above — traffic between your device and Google's AdMob / Play Billing services, governed by Google's policies, not TrenDoc's. TrenDoc does not sell data, because it does not collect any to sell.

08Children's privacy

TrenDoc is a general-audience utility app not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details in §11 and we'll address it.

09Your choices

  • Remove the ad banner entirely via the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase on Home — this also stops the AdMob SDK from requesting ads at all.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising at the OS level in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device, which limits what Google's ad network can use your advertising ID for, independent of TrenDoc.
  • Revoke a file's access at any time — since TrenDoc only ever holds a one-time SAF grant, there is nothing broader to revoke in Android's permission settings.

10Changes to this policy

If TrenDoc's data practices change — for example, if a future version adds a new feature that touches the network — this page will be updated and the "Effective" date at the top will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
